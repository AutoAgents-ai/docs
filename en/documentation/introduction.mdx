---
title: "Product Introduction"
---

The Agents Platform is an intelligent application-building platform designed for enterprises, teams, and individual creators, dedicated to making complex system development lighter, more efficient, and more controllable. Users don't need to write code — they simply organize processes and configure pages and data around business goals to quickly build digital applications suited for management, operations, collaboration, and presentation scenarios. The platform organically combines forms, pages, permissions, processes, and data relationships, helping business staff turn scattered ideas into clear application structures and participate deeply in the entire process from concept to launch, forming a complete closed loop. Compared with the traditional model of repeated requirement communication, scheduled development, and testing before launch, the Agents Platform lets teams quickly generate a runnable version first, continuously adjust it in real usage, and flexibly iterate through configuration whenever requirements change — keeping the system always aligned with the pace of the business. For enterprises, it helps advance business digitalization, standardize processes, and improve collaboration efficiency; for startup teams and individuals, it lowers the barrier to validating ideas, building tools, and showcasing results. The Agents Platform is committed to turning technical capability into an intuitive building experience, helping ideas land faster and letting business grow on its own.
