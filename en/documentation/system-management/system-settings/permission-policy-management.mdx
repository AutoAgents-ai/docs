### Feature Overview

Permission policy management allows administrators to define permission policies for Agents, knowledge bases, custom tools, MCP, models, and databases, controlling whether users can view and use Agents, knowledge bases, and tools uploaded by other users or organizations.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/documentation/ch7_7.6.2.1_0357.png" alt="Permission Policy Management" />
</Frame>

### Use Cases

Scenarios where Agents, knowledge bases, custom tools, MCP, models, or databases require complex permission configuration.

### Accessing the Feature

Click the [System Management \> Permission Policy Management] menu in the left navigation to enter the Permission Policy Management page.

### Operation Steps

### Add a Permission Policy

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/documentation/ch7_7.6.2.6_0360.png" alt="Permission Policy Management" />
</Frame>

Click the Add Permission Policy button to enter the add permission policy page.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/documentation/ch7_7.6.2.6_0361.png" alt="Permission Policy Management" />
</Frame>

Fill in the policy name and select the data type (Agent, Knowledge Base, Custom Tool, MCP, Model, or Database).

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/documentation/ch7_7.6.2.6_0362.png" alt="Permission Policy Management" />
</Frame>

Configure the policy settings (Department, User, Role)
Department: Which departments can use the Agents, knowledge bases, custom tools, MCP, models, or databases that this policy applies to.
User: Which users can use the Agents, knowledge bases, custom tools, MCP, models, or databases that this policy applies to.
Role: Which roles can use the Agents, knowledge bases, custom tools, MCP, models, or databases that this policy applies to.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/documentation/ch7_7.6.2.6_0363.png" alt="Permission Policy Management" />
</Frame>

When assigning users, you can configure Read, Write, and View Usage Records permissions for each user as needed.
Edit a Permission Policy

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/documentation/ch7_7.6.2.6_0364.png" alt="Permission Policy Management" />
</Frame>

Click the edit link in the list to enter the edit permission policy page. Following the same steps described for adding a permission policy, modify the elements as needed, then click the Confirm button to complete the edit.
Delete a Permission Policy

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/documentation/ch7_7.6.2.6_0365.png" alt="Permission Policy Management" />
</Frame>

Click the delete link in the list to delete the permission policy.
