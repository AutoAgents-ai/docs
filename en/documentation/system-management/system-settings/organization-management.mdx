### Feature Overview

Supports batch import of an enterprise's organizational structure, and allows creating departments level by level under a parent organization (with unlimited hierarchy depth supported). Department information includes name, person in charge, phone number, and email, which can be modified at any time after creation, and is available for use by modules such as user management and role permissions.

### Accessing the Feature

Log in to the Agents platform, click the "System Management" button on the left side of the homepage, then click the "Organization Management" module.

### Interface Overview

### Organization Management

Department Name: The name of the organization / department on the platform, supporting a multi-level tree hierarchy (e.g., Organization \> Operations \> IT Department \> IT Department Team 1), used to implement hierarchical permission isolation, data statistics, and resource management.
Manager Name: The name of the person responsible for managing the department / organization, used to clarify management responsibility and ownership.
Phone Number: The contact phone number of the department manager, used for urgent communication, notifications, or account security verification.
Email: The contact email of the department manager, used to receive platform system notifications, permission change alerts, and other emails.
Creation Time: The timestamp when the organization / department was created on the platform, used to trace the establishment and change timeline of the organizational structure.
Edit: Allows modifying the department manager's information, contact details, and other basic settings to update organizational information.
Add: Adds a sub-department under the current department, used to expand and build a multi-level organizational structure.
Delete: Deletes the current department (typically requires that there are no associated users, resources, or sub-departments, to avoid data loss or permission conflicts).
