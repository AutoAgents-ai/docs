---
title: "Data Dashboard"
---

Supports configuring privacy keyword interception (blocking specific user input) in Agent settings, and linking to a preset sensitive word list, safeguarding enterprise information security and compliance requirements at the conversation entry point.
View the creator, creation date, and invocation volume from the Agent dimension; drill down to the specific question details of individual accounts from the user dimension, enabling multi-level analysis from the global view down to the individual level.

### Feature Entry

Log in to the Agents platform and click the "Data Dashboard" button on the left side of the homepage.
