title: "Global Operating Metrics"
---

PV (Page Views): Each time a logged-in user loads or refreshes a page, the PV count increases by one. Repeated refreshes by the same user will continue to accumulate PV.
UV (Unique Visitors): The number of distinct users who accessed the platform during the statistical period; a user who visits multiple times is counted only once.
DAU (Daily Active Users): The number of active users who logged in and used the platform on a given day during the statistical period, reflecting the platform's daily user activity.
MAU (Monthly Active Users): The number of active users who logged in and used the platform during a given month within the statistical period, reflecting the platform's monthly user activity.
Total Agents: The total number of AI Agents (or bot applications) that have been created/configured on the platform, representing the scale of AI services deployed on the platform.
Total Token Consumption: The total number of Tokens (text-processing units) consumed—both input and output—during user interactions with AI Agents within the statistical period, which is typically closely tied to model invocation costs.
Average Daily Conversations: The average number of daily dialogue interactions initiated between users and the platform's AI Agents during the statistical period, reflecting how frequently users engage with AI services.
Departmental Interaction Volume: The number/proportion of interactions between users from different departments/organizations and the platform's AI Agents, used to analyze the usage distribution across business units.
User Activity Trend: Toggle between MAU and DAU.
Page Traffic Trend: Toggle between PV and UV.
