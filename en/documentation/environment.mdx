title: "Runtime Environment"
---

### Access Method

Users access the unified entry point of the Agents Platform through a browser to carry out related operations. Before first use, confirm that the account has been activated, the organizational affiliation has been configured, and roles and resource permissions have been assigned.

### Device and Network Requirements

To ensure a good experience for page rendering, file uploads, workflow orchestration, application preview, and publishing/access, it is recommended to access the platform from a stable office network using mainstream desktop office devices and a recent version of a web browser.

### Preparation Before Use

It is recommended that administrators complete configuration of organizations, users, roles, permission policies, model resources, and system parameters in advance; that business configuration staff organize business goals, process steps, page structure, and data sources; and that general users clarify their access entry point and scope of use.
