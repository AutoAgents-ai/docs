### Overview

Put simply, this feature determines "who can see what data and who can use what functionality." In team collaboration, issuing these "passes" appropriately both protects sensitive data from leaking and lets everyone stick to their own responsibilities, making everyone's work more secure.
Where do you find the permissions page?
It's split into two pages: "Created by Me" and "Granted to Me." Think of them simply as "passes I've issued to others" and "passes others have issued to me."

### The "Created by Me" Page

This page is your personal "control panel." Every permission rule you've set up yourself lives here. From here you can create new rules, modify existing ones, or grant permissions to other people.
Viewing the list: The large table in the middle of the page shows all the rules you've created, clearly listing:
Policy name: The name of the rule (e.g. "Sales Department View Permission").
Data type: The category of data the rule governs.
Policy description: A detailed explanation of the rule, so you can remember what it's for later.
Created at: When the rule was created (down to the year, month, day, hour, minute, and second).
Finding rules & creating new ones: Too many rules to find the one you need? Just search by name in the search box at the top. To add a brand-new rule, click the blue "Add" button to get started.
Common actions: The rightmost column of the list is labeled "Actions," offering three commonly used functions:
Edit: Realized you typed the name wrong, or want to change who it applies to? Click here to modify it. In "Policy Settings," you can select which departments, people, or roles can use it, just like adding members to a group chat. If you want everyone in the company to be able to use it, simply check "Visible to All" at the very bottom for one-click coverage.

Assign usage rights (grant to others): Once a rule is created, it only takes effect once you've granted it to specific people in this step. Similarly, you can pick particular departments, people, or roles, or take the shortcut and select "Visible to All" at the bottom to grant the permission to everyone.

Delete: If a rule is permanently retired, click here to delete it. (Tip: the system will ask you to confirm before deleting — be careful not to delete something by mistake!)

### The "Granted to Me" Page

This page holds the permissions that others (such as your manager or colleagues) have granted to you. Note that this page is read-only — think of it as a display case: you can look, but you can't modify or delete anything here.
Finding rules and viewing the list: If you've been granted too many permissions, you can also search using the box at the top. The table below clearly lists the policy name, data type, policy description, and the date it was created for you.
Viewing details: The rightmost "Actions" column in the list only has a "View" button. Click it to see the full details of that permission (for example, which other departments or people it was also granted to, or whether it's set to "Visible to All").
