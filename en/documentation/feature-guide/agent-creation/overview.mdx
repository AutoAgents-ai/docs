---
title: "Creating an Agent"
---

Creating an Agent is the starting point for building any business capability on the platform. Building on existing Workflow 2.0 assets, the platform lets you construct Agent capabilities through workflow orchestration, model integration, knowledge augmentation, and tool invocation.
- Clarify the Agent's business goals, target users, and expected output.
- Choose the appropriate model resources and complete parameter and permission configuration.
- Use Workflow 2.0 to orchestrate process nodes, data flow, and control flow.
- Bind knowledge sources, tools, or other external capabilities as needed to enhance processing results.
- Complete debugging, validation, and publishing before opening the Agent up to business users.
