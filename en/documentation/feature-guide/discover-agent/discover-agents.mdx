---
title: "Discover Agents"
---

After entering the Agents Platform, go to "Discover Agents" in the left-hand navigation menu to find the "Agent Marketplace." It comes preloaded with a selection of sample Agents covering multiple industries and scenarios, making it easy to learn from and try out. You can filter by category tags to find sample Agents that match your interests.
