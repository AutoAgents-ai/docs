### Feature Overview

Once a Skill has been debugged, you can choose to save it (keep it locally) or publish it (push it to SkillHub). A published Skill can then be directly referenced by a wide range of objects on the platform, such as agents and applications, enabling cross-scenario reuse and standardized distribution of specialized AI capabilities.

### Entry Point

Log in to the Agents platform, click the Skill Management menu on the left side of the home page, select the Skill Details tab, and click the Save or Publish button to deploy or distribute the skill.
