---
title: "Skill Management"
---

Skill Management corresponds to the SkillHub capability, supporting enterprise-level skill sharing, publishing, discovery, downloading, and governance. Based on the current material, the SkillHub page mainly consists of three sections: "Explore Skills," "My Published Skills," and "Documentation."
- In "Explore Skills," browse reusable skill resources.
- In "My Published Skills," manage skill collections published by your team or yourself.
- In "Documentation," view skill introductions, the development process, and guidance on publishing and downloading.
