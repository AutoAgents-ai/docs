title: "App List and Management"
---

On the "My Apps" page, users can view and manage all of their app card assets in one place, and perform basic search and information maintenance.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/documentation/ch6_6.4.2_0138.png" alt="App List and Management" />
</Frame>

### Interface Overview

Viewing and searching the list:
App list: Displays all generated apps as cards, including the app name, creator information, and last edited time.
Search box: Located in the top-right corner of the page, supports fuzzy search of app assets by keyword.
Editing and managing basic information:
"..." More menu: Click the more icon in the top-right corner of the card to open the management menu.
Edit: Supports modifying the name and description of an existing app.
Delete: Supports removing app assets that are no longer needed from the platform.

### Operations

Re-editing the page/logic: For an existing app, click the main body of the card in the list to re-enter the app builder workbench, where you can modify the page layout or binding logic at any time.
Modify name/description: Click "..." -> "Edit" in the top-right corner of the card, update the app's basic information in the dialog, and save.
