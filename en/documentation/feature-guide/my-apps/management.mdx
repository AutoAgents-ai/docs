title: "My Apps Management"
---

In enterprise digital transformation, native AI Agents typically exist as a "chat box"—powerful, but limited to a single mode of interaction, and difficult to embed directly into complex business processes. The "My Apps" module is an app-level packaging hub designed specifically to address this pain point.
Its core value lies in:
Form transformation: Converting complex backend Agent logic into an intuitive, structured frontend app interface, upgrading from "ask and answer" to "click and interact."
Cost reduction and efficiency gains: Business users can "direct" the system to generate a page layout that matches their business logic using natural language, without writing any code, greatly shortening the app development cycle.
End-to-end closed loop: By deeply binding the UI to the underlying Agent, this creates a complete business loop of "frontend interaction—backend logic processing—frontend result presentation," truly turning AI into a productivity tool tailored for the enterprise.

### Entry Point

Log in to the Agents Platform and click "My Apps" in the left-hand primary navigation menu to enter the app management and building page.
