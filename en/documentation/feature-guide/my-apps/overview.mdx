---
title: "My Apps"
---

The "My Apps" module is used to package Agent capabilities into visual apps, and is one of the features through which customers most directly experience the platform's value. Based on the available source material, this module mainly covers four types of operations: app list management, app creation, natural-language page generation, and app publishing.
- Go to the "My Apps" page to view existing apps and search by keyword.
- Click "Create App" and fill in the app name and description.
- Select and bind a target Agent to establish the mapping between the app and its underlying AI capability.
- In the editing workbench, describe the desired page in natural language, then preview and fine-tune it.
- Once you're satisfied with the result, publish the app to obtain an independent access URL.
