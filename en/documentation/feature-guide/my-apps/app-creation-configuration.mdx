---
title: "App Creation and Basic Configuration"
---

The app creation process supports building from scratch, establishing an "identity" and a "brain" for the app through basic information setup and Agent binding.

### Steps

Step 1: Trigger creation. Click "Create App" (the "+" card) at the front of the list page, then select "Create from Blank."

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/documentation/ch6_6.4.3.1_0139.png" alt="App Creation and Basic Configuration" />
</Frame>

Step 2: Basic configuration. In the dialog, enter the "App Name" and "App Description," then click "Next."

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/documentation/ch6_6.4.3.1_0140.png" alt="App Creation and Basic Configuration" />
</Frame>

Step 3: Bind an Agent. In the "Add Agent" window, select a target Agent from the "Created by Me" or "All" list. All of the app's functionality is ultimately powered by the bound Agent. Click "Confirm" when done to enter the editing workbench.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/documentation/ch6_6.4.3.1_0141.png" alt="App Creation and Basic Configuration" />
</Frame>
