---
title: "Publish an App"
---

Once all adjustments are complete, users can publish the app to obtain an independent access entry point.

### Steps

Step 1: Publish. After confirming the preview looks correct, click the blue "Publish" button in the top-right corner of the workbench.
Step 2: Get the link. After a successful publish, the system will display a dedicated link window. Click the copy icon next to the link to obtain the app's independent access URL.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/documentation/ch6_6.4.5.1_0145.png" alt="Publish an App" />
</Frame>

Step 3: Update published content. If you later make changes to the app in the workbench, simply click "Publish" again and click "Update" in the dialog to sync the changes to the published link.
