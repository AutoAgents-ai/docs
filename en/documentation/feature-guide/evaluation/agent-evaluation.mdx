title: "Agent Evaluation"
---

During the development and iteration of an Agent, the randomness and unpredictability of its output are often the core bottleneck that keeps it from reaching production. Without a standardized way to measure quality, the optimization process becomes a kind of "blind-box" debugging. The Agent Evaluation module is an industrial-grade evaluation engine built specifically to solve this problem.
Its core value lies in:
From "random" to "deterministic": Supports batch, concurrent execution of large volumes of test cases, moving away from fragmented, one-off point-to-point debugging and enabling an engineering-grade leap from "spot checks" to "comprehensive quality inspection."
Multi-dimensional, precise measurement: Deeply integrates a response-accuracy evaluation system that automatically benchmarks semantic consistency, logical completeness, and business compliance, turning subjective impressions into objective, quantifiable data metrics.
Data-driven iteration: Provides side-by-side comparisons of results across multiple versions, clearly showing the gains before and after parameter tuning or prompt optimization, and forming a continuous "test - analyze - optimize" improvement loop that makes the evolution of AI capabilities clearly visible.

### Feature Entry

Log in to the Agents Platform, and in the left-hand primary navigation menu click "Evaluation" to expand management of Evaluation Datasets, Evaluation Rules, and Evaluation Tasks.
