---
title: "Permission Assignment and Management"
---

For Agents under "Created by Me," the creator can grant precise access to other members within the organization, enabling secure and controlled distribution of enterprise-grade tools.

### Steps

Step 1: In the "Created by Me" list, locate the target Agent card and click the "..." button in the lower-right corner to open the extended actions menu.
Step 2: Select "Assign Usage Permissions" from the menu to open the "Assign Usage Permissions" configuration window.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/documentation/ch6_6.3.4.1_0132.png" alt="Permission Assignment and Management" />
</Frame>

Step 3: At the top of the dialog, switch between the "Department," "User," "Role," or "Permission Policy" tabs as needed to precisely locate the target for authorization.
Step 4: Select the corresponding items in the organizational structure tree or search bar on the left; the selected items will be displayed in the list on the right.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/documentation/ch6_6.3.4.1_0133.png" alt="Permission Assignment and Management" />
</Frame>

Step 5: Click "Confirm" in the lower-right corner to complete the authorization. The authorized user will then see this Agent in their "Shared with Me" list.
