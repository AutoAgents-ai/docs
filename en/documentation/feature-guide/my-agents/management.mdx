---
title: "My Agents Management"
---

"My Agents" is the central hub for managing all Agent assets under the current account. It provides convenient category-based search, status maintenance, permission assignment, and usage data tracking, helping users manage the full lifecycle of an Agent from configuration and distribution to data analysis.

### Entry Point

Log in to the Agents Platform and click "My Agents" in the left-hand primary navigation menu to open the management page.
