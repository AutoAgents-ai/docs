title: "Basic Agent Management"
---

This module provides quick maintenance functions for Agent cards, including sort optimization and asset cleanup.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/documentation/ch6_6.3.3_0131.png" alt="Basic Agent Management" />
</Frame>

### Operations

Pin to top: Click the "Pin" button in the lower-left corner of the card. Once pinned, the Agent will always appear first in the list, regardless of its creation time. Click again to unpin.
Re-editing the orchestration: Click the main body of the card (or its name) to jump directly to that Agent's basic configuration and flow orchestration page.
Delete an asset:
Click the "..." (More) button in the lower-right corner of the card.
Select "Delete" from the dropdown menu.
Click "Confirm" in the confirmation dialog to permanently remove the Agent from the platform.
