---
title: "My Agents"
---

"My Agents" is used to centrally manage Agent resources that you have created or been authorized to use, typically covering operations such as viewing, editing, debugging, publishing, and permission adjustments.
- Quickly search for Agents by name or update time.
- Open a target Agent to maintain its Prompts, flow, model, and resource bindings.
- Verify output quality through single-node debugging and full runs.
- Assign the scope of use by department, user, role, or permission policy.
