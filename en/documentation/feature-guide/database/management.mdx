A database is a system specifically designed for storing and querying data. It is designed to connect databases, break down data silos, and make it easy to query and analyze multiple data sources, empowering enterprises to make data-driven, intelligent decisions. With the platform, you can:
Intelligently process and analyze large volumes of data:
Fill knowledge gaps in large models: Supplement the blind spots of large models for domain-specific needs, expanding the coverage of agents.
Improve the accuracy and professionalism of responses: Significantly improve the response quality and professionalism of agents across various scenarios through rich knowledge base support.

### Use Cases

Customer service Q&A: Respond to customer inquiries quickly and accurately, improving customer satisfaction.
Product consultation: Provide users with detailed product information, boosting purchase confidence.
Scenarios requiring standardized responses: Ensure accurate information delivery and consistent brand image in situations that demand consistency and authority.
With professional guidance from the knowledge base, agents can provide users with consistent and authoritative information, ensuring high-quality communication and effectively maintaining the enterprise's brand image.

### Entry Point

Click the "Database" menu in the left sidebar of the platform to access the data management page.
