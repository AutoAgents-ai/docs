### Role Breakdown

| Role | Main Responsibilities | Typical Operations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Platform administrator | Responsible for basic platform configuration, resource onboarding, permission governance, and operational maintenance | Organization management, user management, role management, model configuration, dictionary management, security log review |
| Business configuration staff | Build Agents, processes, and applications around business goals | Create Agents, configure workflows, bind knowledge sources, generate application pages, publish applications |
| General user | Use the platform's existing capabilities within their authorized scope and provide usage feedback | Access Agents, use applications, view results, submit feedback |

### Usage Flow Overview

Platform usage generally follows the process of "resource preparation, capability building, application publishing, user usage, and continuous optimization." Administrators first complete resource and permission preparation, business configuration staff then carry out Agent, workflow, and application building, general users access and use the platform within their authorized scope, and responsible parties subsequently perform continuous optimization based on logs, feedback, and data metrics.

### Permission and Security Principles

The platform controls the visible and usable scope of resources through a combination of authorization by organization, role, personnel, and permission policy. For shared resources such as models, knowledge, tools, MCP, and databases, the principles of least privilege, on-demand access, and auditability should be followed, together with mechanisms such as log auditing, usage monitoring, and sensitive-word governance to safeguard secure platform usage.
